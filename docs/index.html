Welcome to Government Degree College Madyan Swat

Established in 2006 to serve the youth of Upper Swat, GDC Madyan provides high-quality public-sector education with state-of-the-art laboratory infrastructure, sports grounds, and dedicated faculty.

BISE Swat Affiliated

Intermediate HSSC Programs

Two-year rigorous foundation programs in F.Sc Pre-Medical, F.Sc Pre-Engineering, FCS / ICS Computer Science, and FA Humanities preparing students for professional university entrance tests and careers.

Explore Intermediate
University of Swat

Undergraduate BS 4-Year Programs

Eight-semester comprehensive degree programs in BS Computer Science, BS Political Science, and BS English under the HEC semester system with advanced software labs, seminar rooms, and research projects.

Explore BS Programs

HED & HEC Scholarships

Dedicated financial support through KP Chief Minister Educational Endowment Fund, HEC Need-Based Aid, Mora Scholarships, and College Merit Fee Concessions ensuring no talented student is left behind.

Aerial Master Layout

Purpose-Built Infrastructure in Upper Swat

Captured at a 90-degree drone elevation, the master plan of Government Degree College Madyan demonstrates an integrated architectural layout featuring symmetrical red-brick academic wings, a centralized botanical garden, administrative offices, and spacious outdoor sports grounds.

  • Multi-story main academic block with airy, naturally lit lecture halls
  • Dedicated science and computer laboratories wing
  • Secure gated perimeter with mountain views of Bahrain & Miandam
Eco-Friendly Campus

Manicured Star-and-Crescent Green Lawns

The central quadrangle features a renowned star-and-crescent shaped botanical hedge topiary surrounded by floral borders and pine trees. Students utilize these serene green grounds for quiet study, peer discussions, and outdoor academic symposiums.

GDC Madyan participates annually in the KP Plant for Pakistan Green Drive, maintaining over 1,000 indigenous trees and ornamental shrubs.
Modern Academic Facilities

Wide Pillared Verandas & Advanced Labs

Designed to blend classical Pakistani academic architecture with alpine weather requirements, the college verandas connect high-speed fiber-optic computer labs, biology dissecting rooms, chemistry wet labs, and physics demonstration halls.
